# NeroSecurity Wiki

Player- and contributor-facing documentation for **NeroSecurity**, part of the Neroland ecosystem.
Built on **Neroland Core**.

NeroSecurity lets you protect a base. A **Security Hub** controls an **installation**: who its owners
and members are, which devices answer to it, and whether it is in lockdown. Doors, chests, keycards,
laser grids, cameras, alarms and force fields all ask the hub's installation the same question: is
this player allowed here? The server answers it every time, and any refusal wins.

Bases stay raidable. Anyone can break every device except the hub, force fields overload under fire, and
nothing makes a door unbreakable. Owners are never locked out of their own base.

> **Status:** version `0.5.0-beta.1`, a beta. Some textures are still simple placeholders.
