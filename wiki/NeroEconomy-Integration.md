# NeroEconomy integration

NeroSecurity works with or without **NeroEconomy**. When NeroEconomy is installed, higher security tiers
can cost credits, paid into NeroEconomy's Reserve.

These promises hold whatever security you use:

- **You can always reach your own economy blocks.** Your Player Shop, Auto-Trader and Market Board
  listings stay open to you, and Bank, Trading and Exchange Terminals stay usable, even if a paid tier
  lapses.
- **Security never breaks your blocks** to enforce a rule.
- **Security never looks at your balance** or holdings to decide who gets in.
- **You are only charged when you choose** a tier or renew it. If a renewal cannot be paid, your
  protection drops to a lower tier; you are never locked out of your own blocks.

Without NeroEconomy, tiers are free or set up another way by the server.

## Privacy

NeroSecurity records nothing about who paid for which tier. NeroEconomy keeps its own ledger, which you can
export or have erased along with your other economy data.
